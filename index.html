01 / The problem

Healthcare information has a future.

Medical information is powerful — but only when people can actually organize it, understand it, and use it responsibly.

Every year, healthcare generates an overwhelming volume of information: laboratory reports, medical records, personal health measurements, research data, years of history scattered across paper files and disconnected systems. Most of it is hard to read, harder to search, and impossible to act on without help.

ZADE MedCore is building the technology layer that connects that information to the people who need it — turning scattered documents into digital experiences that are organized, understandable, and genuinely useful.

We're not building a replacement for doctors or laboratory scientists. We're building better tools for the people already doing that work — and for the individuals trying to understand their own health.

02 / The ecosystem

Three intelligence pillars.

The foundation of everything ZADE MedCore is building.

Laboratory Intelligence

Helping people interact better with their laboratory information.

  • Organize laboratory results in one place
  • Plain-language explanations of medical terminology
  • Store and revisit past investigations
  • Track changes across results over time
  • Educational resources for students and patients

Medical Data Intelligence

Turning healthcare information into organized digital knowledge.

  • Personal health records, kept in one system
  • Structured organization of medical data
  • Fast search and filtering
  • Clear visualization of health information
  • Analytics tools, in development

Personal Health Intelligence

Helping individuals understand their own health journey.

  • Blood pressure tracking
  • Heart rate over time
  • Glucose monitoring
  • Symptom logging
  • General health measurements, all in one view
